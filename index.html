Human Body Systems & Interactive Guide

Human Anatomy Explorer

There are 12 major anatomy systems in the human body. Select a system below to learn more about its organs, structures, and functions.

🦴

Skeletal System

Includes all of the bones and joints in the body that provide structure and protection.
💪

Muscular System

Responsible for the movement of the human body and maintaining posture.
❤️

Cardiovascular System

Consists of the heart and blood vessels that transport oxygen and nutrients.
🥗

Digestive System

Converts food into energy and basic nutrients to feed the entire body.
🧠

Nervous System

Consists of the brain, spinal cord, and nerves that control body activities.
🫁

Respiratory System

Provides oxygen to the body's cells while removing carbon dioxide.
🧬

Endocrine System

Includes glands that produce hormones to regulate metabolism and growth.
🛡️

Immune & Lymphatic System

Defends the body against infection, diseases, and foreign invaders.
🩺

Urinary System

Filters excess fluid and waste products from the bloodstream.
🩹

Integumentary System

Consists of skin, hair, nails, and glands that protect the body.
♀️

Female Reproductive System

Includes organs responsible for producing eggs and offspring development.
♂️

Male Reproductive System

Includes organs responsible for producing sperm and male hormones.
