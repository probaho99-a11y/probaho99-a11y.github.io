Human Organs

🫀 Heart

The heart pumps blood throughout the body and supplies oxygen to every organ.

🧠 Brain

The brain controls thinking, memory, movement, emotions and all body functions.

🫁 Lungs

The lungs bring oxygen into the body and remove carbon dioxide.

🫀 Liver

The liver filters blood, stores nutrients and removes harmful substances.

🫘 Kidney

The kidneys filter waste from blood and produce urine.

🦴 Skeleton

The skeleton supports the body and protects internal organs.
